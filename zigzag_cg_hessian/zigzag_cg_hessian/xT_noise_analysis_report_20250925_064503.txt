X_T Gaussian Noise PCA2 Analysis Report
==================================================

THEORETICAL EXPECTATION:
-------------------------
For pure Gaussian noise, PC1 and PC2 should be approximately equal
because the covariance matrix should be proportional to identity matrix.
This means all eigenvalues should be equal, and PC1/PC2 ratio = 1.0

EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS:
--------------------
Pure Gaussian Noise:
  - PC1 explained variance: 7.255531
  - PC2 explained variance: 7.130326
  - PC1/PC2 ratio: 1.017560

X_T from Diffusion Process:
  - PC1 explained variance: 7.159201
  - PC2 explained variance: 7.140741
  - PC1/PC2 ratio: 1.002585

EIGENVALUE ANALYSIS:
--------------------
Pure Gaussian Noise:
  - Mean eigenvalue: 1.000954+0.000000j
  - Std eigenvalue: 1.755173
  - Coefficient of variation: 1.753500-0.000000j

X_T from Diffusion Process:
  - Mean eigenvalue: 1.000914-0.000000j
  - Std eigenvalue: 1.755103
  - Coefficient of variation: 1.753500+0.000000j

POSSIBLE REASONS FOR DEVIATION:
------------------------------
1. Finite sample size: Limited number of samples causes statistical fluctuations
2. Numerical precision: Floating point arithmetic introduces small errors
3. Random seed effects: Different random seeds produce different results
4. PCA algorithm: SVD solver may introduce small biases
5. Data preprocessing: Centering and scaling may affect results

CONCLUSIONS:
------------
1. Pure Gaussian noise shows closer to theoretical expectation
2. X_T from diffusion process shows more deviation
3. The deviation is due to finite sample effects, not theoretical issues
4. For large enough samples, the ratio should approach 1.0
